# Outing Logistics & Safety Checklist — Greetland Community Group

This master checklist details all operational, travel, equipment, and safety considerations for our community volunteer outing from Greetland to Northern England's national parks.

---

## 1. Pre-Outing Coordination (4–6 Weeks Prior)
- [ ] **Liaison with National Park Authority / Host Organisation:**
  - Peak District National Park Authority Ranger Service / Moors for the Future, OR
  - Yorkshire Dales National Park Authority (Dales Volunteers), OR
  - National Trust Marsden Moor / South Pennines team.
- [ ] **Confirm Activity Scope & Site Risk Assessment:**
  - Verify working area terrain (moorland peat, river valley, woodland).
  - Review weather contingency plan (indoor briefing/shelter availability).
- [ ] **Insurance & Permissions:**
  - Check group public liability coverage.
  - Verify if under-18s are attending and ensure parental consent forms are collected.
- [ ] **Community Group Poll:**
  - Finalize outing date and park choice based on signups in Issue #1.

---

## 2. Transport & Journey Management (1–2 Weeks Prior)
- [ ] **Meeting Location:**
  - Primary: Greetland Community Centre Car Park (Rochdale Road, Greetland, HX4 8JG).
  - Alternative pickup: West Vale public car park.
- [ ] **Carpool Logistics:**
  - Finalize vehicle driver-to-passenger pairings.
  - Plan convoy / independent travel routes (avoid heavy congestion times on M62 or A629).
  - Collect fuel contributions (£5–£10 per passenger recommended).
- [ ] **Minibus Alternative (if >14 volunteers):**
  - Obtain quote from Calderdale Community Transport or local minibus hire.
  - Confirm designated certified driver.
- [ ] **Parking & Base Access:**
  - Confirm parking coordinates / What3Words location for the ranger station / trailhead.
  - Verify parking payment method (coins, RingGo, National Trust membership, or ranger permit).

---

## 3. Volunteer Personal Kit Checklist (To communicate to attendees)
- [ ] **Footwear:** Broken-in walking boots with ankle support and deep vibram/lug tread, or steel-toe gumboots for wet peat work.
- [ ] **Weatherproof Outerwear:** Waterproof jacket (taped seams) and waterproof overtrousers.
- [ ] **Clothing Layers:**
  - Base layer (moisture-wicking, non-cotton).
  - Mid layer (fleece or wool sweater).
  - Quick-drying trekking trousers (strictly NO jeans/denim).
  - Spare warm hat and sun hat.
  - Thermal neck buff.
- [ ] **Gloves:**
  - 1 pair heavy-duty puncture-resistant work gloves (rigger or nitrile grip).
  - 1 pair warm fleece gloves.
- [ ] **Rucksack & Dry Protection:**
  - 20–30L daypack with waterproof liner or rain cover.
- [ ] **Sustenance:**
  - Substantial packed lunch (sandwiches, pork pies, flapjacks, nuts, fruit).
  - 1.5L to 2L drinking water.
  - 500ml–1L insulated thermos flask with tea/coffee/hot soup.
- [ ] **Personal Medical Kit:**
  - Personal inhalers, blister plasters, personal prescription medicines.
  - High SPF sunscreen and lip balm.

---

## 4. Group Equipment & Safety Supplies
- [ ] **First Aid & Health:**
  - Outdoor comprehensive First Aid kit (BS 8599-1 compliant).
  - Tick removal tools (Tick Twister) and antiseptic wipes.
  - Eyewash pods (crucial for grit, peat, or woodchips).
  - Emergency foil space blankets and 4–8 person Bothy shelter.
- [ ] **Sanitation & Hygiene:**
  - Hand sanitizer dispensers and wet wipes.
  - Heavy-duty refuse sacks for litter picking and waste disposal.
  - Portable hand-washing station / water container.
- [ ] **Task Tools & Safety Equipment (Coordinated with Rangers):**
  - High-visibility safety vests (orange/yellow).
  - Safety goggles / eye protection.
  - Spades, mattocks, loppers, bow saws, mallet hammers (inspected for loose handles).
  - Hard hats (if working near woodland felling or stone walls).
- [ ] **Communications & Navigation:**
  - Printed Ordnance Survey (OS Explorer) paper map of the project area.
  - Compass and GPS app (OS Maps / What3Words).
  - Whistle (standard alpine emergency signal: 6 blasts per minute).
  - Fully charged mobile phones and power bank.

---

## 5. On-the-Day Operational Checklist
- [ ] **07:30 AM:** Organiser arrives at Greetland Community Centre.
- [ ] **07:45 AM:** Volunteers assemble, attendance taken against Issue #1 roster.
- [ ] **08:00 AM:** Departure from Greetland.
- [ ] **09:15 AM:** Arrival on-site, rendezvous with park rangers.
- [ ] **09:30 AM:** Safety briefing, risk assessment walkthrough, task allocation.
- [ ] **12:30 PM:** Regroup, head count, sanitise hands, lunch break.
- [ ] **01:15 PM:** Afternoon session commences.
- [ ] **03:30 PM:** Work stops, tool clean-down, count back of all equipment.
- [ ] **03:45 PM:** Ranger debrief, volunteer group photo, certificates/acknowledgments.
- [ ] **04:15 PM:** Post-work social (local pub/tea room visit).
- [ ] **05:45 PM:** Safe arrival back in Greetland.

---

## 6. Post-Event Follow-up
- [ ] Clean and store any community group-owned tools and first aid supplies.
- [ ] Record total conservation hours contributed.
- [ ] Post thank-you note and photo gallery on community social media / parish bulletin.
- [ ] Close related GitHub issues and archive logs for future outing planning.
